Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a paid GuardUp plan, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full — no forms, no explanation required.

2. What is refundable

  • First-time subscription payments (monthly or annual) within 14 days.
  • One-time "assisted setup" fees if the setup was not completed within 7 days of purchase.

3. What is not refundable

  • Renewals after the first 14-day period.
  • Consumable third-party costs (e.g. your own server or DNS provider fees) — these are billed by those providers, not us.

4. How to request

Email support@guard.7zi.com with the email address used at purchase. We process refunds through the original payment processor within 3 business days. Funds return to your original payment method per the processor's timeline (typically 5–10 business days).

5. Cancellation

You can cancel any time from your account. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund a past charge. Because the agent runs locally and is open source, canceling your hosted plan never disables your self-hosted protection — the agent keeps working on your server.

6. Questions

We'd rather keep you as a customer than process a refund. If something isn't working, tell us and we'll help fix it first. Contact: support@guard.7zi.com
