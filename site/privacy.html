Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

1. Our core principle

Your DNS credentials never leave your machine. The GuardUp agent runs on your own server, reads credentials from a local file (~/.guardup/credentials.env, mode 600), and communicates directly with your DNS provider. We do not receive, store, or transmit your DNS API tokens.

2. What the open-source agent collects

Nothing is sent to us by the agent. Health-check results and failover state are written to local files on your server. If you enable an optional alert channel (email or Telegram), notifications are sent directly from your server to the destination you configure.

3. What hosted services collect

If you use a paid hosted plan, we collect only what is necessary to operate it:

  • Account data: email address and account identifier from your payment provider.
  • Billing data: handled by our merchant-of-record payment processor. We do not store full card numbers or bank details.
  • Service telemetry: site health status and switch events you opt into sending, used to render your dashboard and reports.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site uses no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers. We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (page counts only, no cross-site profiling) to understand which pages are useful.

5. Data retention and deletion

Hosted account data is retained while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for billing records. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting support@guard.7zi.com. Local agent data stays on your server under your control and is never retrieved by us.

6. Third parties

Payment processing is handled by our merchant of record (Creem / Dodo Payments). Their privacy policies govern the billing data they collect. We do not sell personal data to anyone.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a revised date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@guard.7zi.com
