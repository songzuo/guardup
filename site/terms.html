1. What GuardUp is

GuardUp is a self-hosted software agent that monitors your origin server and, when it detects a failure, updates DNS records with providers you authorize (Cloudflare, Aliyun DNS, DNSPod, Tencent Cloud) to point traffic at a standby server you control. The open-source agent is licensed under AGPL-3.0. Optional hosted services (dashboard, alerts, multi-region confirmation) are provided under these Terms.

2. Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for operating your origin and standby servers, and for maintaining valid DNS provider credentials.
  • You retain full control of your DNS records. GuardUp modifies only the records you explicitly configure.
  • You must have the right to modify the domains you configure. Do not use GuardUp on domains you do not control.
  • DNS credentials are stored locally on your machine. You are responsible for securing that machine and rotating credentials if compromised.

3. Honest service description

GuardUp is a failover tool, not an uptime guarantee. Failover time depends on your DNS provider's TTL settings — typically 1 to 10 minutes. We do not promise instant failover or 100% availability.
